## Important interpretation rule

The course owner defines a **set** as one full-topic question in a four-question part. The website therefore organizes the course into A1–A4 and B1–B4. **2020 is explicitly treated as an exception** and is not reconstructed where the supplied paper does not provide enough evidence.

## Accuracy policy

- Course definitions, methods, year tags and recurring patterns follow the supplied sources.
- Holes/cut-outs are consistently treated as negative area/volume/mass.
- Added arithmetic checks are used only where the supplied data is sufficiently clear.
- Missing or ambiguous source data is not silently invented.
- Mathematical powers/subscripts are typeset using proper notation in the website UI.
